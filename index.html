Is this roadmap suitable for complete beginners?

Yes, absolutely. The roadmap is structured sequentially from Day 1 to Day 60. Even if you have never sent a cold DM or don't have an established agency, you'll learn how to position your current skills, optimize your profile, and initiate conversations with international prospects.

What skills do I need to start?

This playbook works for any digital service skill: web design, software development, video editing, graphic design, content writing, SEO, paid ads, or virtual assistance. The focus is teaching you how to package your skill into a high-ticket offer and sell it to international clients.

Why LinkedIn & Instagram instead of Upwork or Fiverr?

On Upwork and Fiverr, hundreds of freelancers race to the bottom by undercutting each other on price, and the platform takes a 10–20% cut. On LinkedIn and Instagram, you reach decision-makers directly before they post a job. You have zero competition on the conversation and command full market rates.
